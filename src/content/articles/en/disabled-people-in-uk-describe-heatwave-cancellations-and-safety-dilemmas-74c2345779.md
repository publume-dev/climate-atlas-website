Emily Davison, a London-based journalist with a visual impairment, says heat has forced her to cancel journeys because it is often too hot for her guide dog to travel safely, leaving her without earnings. Jimmy Macfarlane, a Cardiff actor who uses a powered wheelchair, says heat drains his wheelchair's battery, and a heat-related lift breakdown once left him stranded in his sixth-floor flat. Claire Sisk, an advocate with distorted vision, keeps her windows closed during hot weather because she cannot see whether someone has entered her property, making standard advice to open them unsafe for her.

Hollye Kirkcaldy, a PhD researcher at the University of Bath, found that official UK heatwave guidance often overlooks disabled people's circumstances and can create new barriers, harming independence and adding financial pressures. Disability campaigner Doug Paulley cancelled physiotherapy and missed giving evidence to the Covid Inquiry during a heatwave because he judged that travelling was unsafe.
