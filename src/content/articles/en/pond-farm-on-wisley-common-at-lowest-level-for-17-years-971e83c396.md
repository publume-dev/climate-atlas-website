According to James Herd, director of reserves management at Surrey Wildlife Trust, the pond at Pond Farm on Wisley Common is at its lowest level in 17 years. The pond's drying up is linked to a period of more than 50 days without rainfall, as recorded by meteorologists at Wisley.

This drop highlights the impact of sustained drought on lowland heathland habitats. In response, Surrey Wildlife Trust is considering introducing beavers to help rewet these habitats and is in ongoing positive conversations with Natural England about a licence.
