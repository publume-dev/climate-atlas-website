The Atlanta City Council passed an ordinance introduced by Council member Liliana Bakhtiari that requires cool roofs throughout the city. Cool roofs are built with highly reflective materials that absorb less heat from the sun.

The ordinance applies to new roofs and major renovations, so widespread adoption will take time. However, models show that within a few decades, cool roofs could reduce temperatures in some of the hottest Atlanta neighborhoods by up to 6 degrees. Residents with cool roofs will experience benefits right away: their homes won't get as hot, and they will spend less on air conditioning.

The source does not specify the exact date of the ordinance's passage, though it says it passed last year.
