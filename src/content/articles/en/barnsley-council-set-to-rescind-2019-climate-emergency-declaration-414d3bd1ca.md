Barnsley Council is set to rescind its 2019 climate emergency declaration. Council leader Brown said the council would continue to back practical work that delivers clear benefits for residents and businesses, including improving energy efficiency and securing external funding.

The decision affects the direction of climate policy in the area, although the council will continue specific environmental work despite the rescission. An initial report on the rescission is due by the end of September, which may influence future policy priorities and resource allocation.

A 2024 report stated the council was not 'on track' to reduce its carbon emissions, which may have served as a backdrop to the decision.

The exact date of the council vote and the specific reasons for rescission beyond the leader's statement are not detailed in the report.
