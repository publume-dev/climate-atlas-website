According to NASA, the twin CubeSats recorded two complete seasonal cycles in the Arctic and Antarctic. PREFIRE measures far-infrared radiation, which accounts for nearly 60 percent of the energy Earth loses to space and had never been comprehensively measured globally before.

NASA states the observations are expected to help improve weather and climate predictions used by industries, national defense, and Arctic communities.

The captured record highlights a seasonal contrast: Arctic summer heat is enough to thaw broad areas of tundra and sea ice, while Antarctic summer temperatures rarely climb above freezing.

The source describes seasonal swings qualitatively and does not report quantitative temperature changes or uncertainty estimates.
