The program includes heat-limiting street coatings, tree planting, shaded bus shelters, home weatherization, and grants for heat-reflective roofs.

Underinvested neighborhoods in San Antonio have less tree cover and more concrete, and they face higher heat risks.

In 2023, San Antonio recorded 75 days above 100 degrees Fahrenheit.
