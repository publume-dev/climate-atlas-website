On August 3, 2026, NASA's INSPYRE mission completed its first sampling run of the summer, flying through the high-altitude smoke plume from Utah's Widemouth 2 fire at about 12 kilometers above the surface.

The sampling mission aims to study how wildfire plumes affect the atmosphere and climate. The Aqua satellite measured cloud-top brightness temperatures below -40°C on August 2, 2026, indicating cloud tops reached the upper troposphere or stratosphere, which could influence atmospheric chemistry and radiation balance.

On August 2, 2026, the Widemouth 2 fire produced two pyroCb bursts and a rare pre-dawn pyroCb, which is unusual because morning pyroCbs typically require daytime heating to form.

Quantitative measurements of smoke composition or mass injection from the sampling flight are not yet available, which could further constrain the climate impacts.
