Carbon Brief analysis finds that weakening UK zero-emission vehicle (ZEV) targets could cost consumers up to £3bn a year by 2030.

A weaker ZEV mandate could require the UK to import an extra 17 million barrels of oil in 2030, raising net imports by 8%, and add 7.4 million tonnes of CO2, equivalent to 2.5% of national emissions. These effects would primarily impact consumer costs, energy security, and emissions reduction progress.

It is noted that the consultation is reported but not officially confirmed, and details of the proposed target change are based on media reports, introducing uncertainty into the figures.
