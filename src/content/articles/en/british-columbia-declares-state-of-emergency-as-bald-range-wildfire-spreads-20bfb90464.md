The Bald Range wildfire grew to 53 square miles (136 sq km) after breaking out on Friday and remained out of control as of Monday. Police said an 80-year-old woman died on Sunday while trying to flee the fire.

More than 12,000 residents in the affected area remained under evacuation order as of Monday. The Bald Range fire was one of more than 100 fires burning in British Columbia, 70 of which started in the previous week.

Dry conditions in the region are underlined by Okanagan Lake's water level, which was at its lowest since 1992 as of July, according to the Okanagan Basin Water Board.

Officials had not determined how many homes were lost to the Bald Range fire as of Monday, leaving total property damage unknown. The report's broader point that climate change has made wildfires more intense and frequent is attributed only to unnamed experts, without a specific study or dataset.
