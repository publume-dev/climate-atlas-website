On August 12, 2026, NASA astronaut photography showed smoke shrouding the Cascade volcanoes. By that date, the Grasshopper fire near Mount Hood had burned nearly 84,000 acres and prompted 'go now' evacuation orders in Wasco County.

The smoke affected air quality and public safety. On August 4, air quality in Mount Rainier National Park reached unhealthy levels due to elevated PM2.5 from drifting wildfire smoke.

The National Interagency Fire Center outlook indicated above-normal fire potential across the Northwest through August 2026, influenced by warm, dry conditions associated with El Niño.

The cause of the Grand Park 2 fire was undetermined as of August 12, 2026.
