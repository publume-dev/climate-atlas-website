Of the nearly 8 million U.S. affordable housing units the Union of Concerned Scientists analyzed, almost half were in counties that received at least 21 days of extreme-heat alerts from the National Weather Service in 2024.

The analysis covers those nearly 8 million affordable housing units. The Union of Concerned Scientists team recommends policies that address affordability and extreme heat together, such as weatherization and energy bill assistance for low-income people.

The article does not name or link the underlying UCS report or its methodology, so the definitions of affordable housing units and extreme-heat alert days cannot be independently verified from the supplied evidence.
