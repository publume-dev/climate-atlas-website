Carbon Brief's factcheck finds that the Conservative 'alternative policy pathway' would result in an extra 524 million tonnes of CO2 emissions between 2030 and 2050, comparable to South Africa's annual emissions.

The extra emissions are equivalent to South Africa's annual output, underscoring the policy's potential impact on the UK's carbon budgets and climate targets. The report's assumptions could influence policymakers and public understanding of the costs and benefits of the energy transition.

The report also assumes gas prices will fall to pre-conflict levels and remain low for two decades, which Aurora Energy Research calls 'hard to fathom' and at odds with market expectations. Additionally, it assumes gas power plant capital expenditure of £650 per kilowatt, far below recent analyses citing costs over double that, with some US projects reporting $2,000/kW or more.
