The 2024 settlement of Navahine v. Hawai'i DOT requires the Hawai'i Department of Transportation to decarbonize the transportation sector by 2045, meaning zero emissions from ground, marine, and interisland air transportation.

As part of the settlement, the department established the Hawai'i Youth Transportation Council, allowing youth from across Hawaii to provide input on public transit, bike lanes, and other strategies to move away from fossil fuels. The settlement also requires HDOT to draft a greenhouse gas reduction plan with benchmarks for 2030, 2035, and 2040, complete the pedestrian, bicycle, and transit network within five years, and increase EV infrastructure.
