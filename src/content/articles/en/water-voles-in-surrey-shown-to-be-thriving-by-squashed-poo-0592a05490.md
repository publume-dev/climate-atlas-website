A year after 42 water voles were released into the Hogsmill River in Surrey, surveys indicate the population has survived the winter and established both upstream and downstream of the release site. Evidence includes droppings, burrows, and camera trap footage.

This marks the return of water voles to Surrey after more than 20 years of local extinction in the Hogsmill River catchment. The project is coordinated with efforts by the Waterlife Recovery Trust to eradicate non-native mink, which predate on water voles, from Surrey.

The article does not provide specific population estimates or quantitative survey data, only qualitative signs such as droppings, burrows, and camera trap evidence.
