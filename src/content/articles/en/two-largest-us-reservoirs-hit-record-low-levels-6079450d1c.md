On 7 August 2026, Lake Mead's water level dropped to 317.11 metres, a new record low. On 15 August, Lake Powell fell to 1,072.87 metres, also a record low. The two reservoirs are the largest in the US, and their declining levels have significant implications for water supply.

Experts attribute the record-low levels to climate change, population growth, and over-consumption. These factors collectively exacerbate water scarcity, affecting regions that depend on these reservoirs for their water supply.
