On 13 August 2026, temperatures exceeded 37°C in several locations, provisionally setting a new record for the number of days in a year with temperatures of 37°C or above. That day was also the 38th day this year when UK temperatures exceeded 30°C, breaking the previous record of 34 days. The previous day, 12 August, was the first day in August to reach 35°C or more, making it the 4th consecutive month to reach this temperature in 2026.

This summer could become the warmest on record in the UK. As long as August temperatures remain average or above for the month as a whole, this summer will replace 2025 as the warmest on record. On 26 June, Lingwood in Norfolk recorded 38°C, a new UK temperature record for June and the highest reading so far this year.
