Unlimited Potential, a nonprofit in Phoenix, partners with Arizona State University and the city of Phoenix to plant trees in low-income neighborhoods that experience temperatures 10 to 15 degrees Fahrenheit higher than other areas due to heat-absorbing concrete and asphalt.

The program includes a paid 10-week workforce development course in which about 30 residents have graduated, and almost all graduates are now employed in tree care.

The report does not specify the total number of trees planted or the geographic scope of the program.
